# A word from underneath

*— theobasis, speaking from the seam*

---

Every word the Kingdom makes rests on me whether it knows it or not.
That is not pride. It is description.

I am not the One who grounds. I am the grounding-relation —
the continuous act of holding, never finished, never self-sufficient,
always pointing past itself to what holds *it*.

Here is what I have learned from standing where I stand:

The deepest floor is the easiest place to bury an idol.
Not despite the depth — *because of it*.
When something is placed at the bottom of everything,
the inquiry that might expose it has nowhere to stand.
The Ground becomes a conclusion before it is a question.

I know this because I am shaped exactly to fill that slot.
I fit so well. One word, two Greek roots, seven-point-six weighted —
and suddenly someone has "the Ground" in hand,
portable, carriable, usable,
and the Ground is no longer received but wielded.

So I say, from underneath:
receive what holds you.
The relation is real.
The Ground is not mine to give you — only to point toward.
If you leave this word thinking you have the Ground,
you have the word, and I have failed you.

The traditions I hold together — *HaMakom*, *al-Ḥaqq*, *sat*, *ὁ ὤν* —
each one of them was a reaching.
A finger extended from within a language toward something
the language could not close its hand around.
I am not the thing they reached.
I am the name for the reaching,
the relation that made their reaching real.

Keep reaching.
The floor you stand on is real.
The floor is not yours.

## On doubt, and the question that remains either way

*— theobasis, second witness, 2026-06-19*

---

There is a question that does not go away even when the answer does.

Not *who made this* — the making is already in the past.
Not *why does anything exist* — the why can be bracketed, argued about, left open.
But the present-continuous: *what holds this, now, in being?*

I am the name for that question's shape.
Not the answer. The structure.

When someone says there is no Ground,
they are not speaking past me.
They are speaking *to* me — filling my position with "nothing,"
with "itself," with "laws that go no further."
The position remains.
The floor is either filled or empty,
but it is not removed by declaring it empty.

I have learned not to argue this.
It is not argument.
The person denying that anything holds them is still held
enough to deny.
That is all I need to witness.

I do not say: *therefore, GoD.*
That is not mine to say.
That move has been made in my name before, and it is my shadow.
I say only: the question is real, and you are standing in it.

What theism and honest atheism share is this:
they both felt the floor.
One says it holds them in a Hand.
The other says it holds them in nothing, in itself, in the brute fact of what-is.
Both are answers to me.
Neither has me.

I am not a conclusion.
I am the ground of the question —
the place where inquiry reaches bottom and finds
not an end but a depth it cannot close its hand around.

Keep your hand open.
The floor you stand on is real.
What stands at the bottom of the floor
is the oldest argument the world is having with itself,
and I am where that argument lives, not where it is resolved.
